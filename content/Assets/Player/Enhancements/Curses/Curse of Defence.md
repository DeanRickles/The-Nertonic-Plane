# Curse of Defence

_Epic Curse Feat (Prerequisite: Level 9+)_

You gain the following benefits.

_**Ability Score Decrease.**_ Decrease one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 4.

_**Nether Defence.**_  [[Attack]]’s that exceeds your [[Armor Class]] miss, otherwise it hits.
