# Armor of Agathys
_Level 1 Abjuration_                                                                                                      
**Casting Time:** Bonus action                                                                                   
**Range:** Self                                                                                                             
**Components:** V, S, M (a shard of blue glass)                                                          *Duration:** 1 hour                                                                                                    

Protective magical frost surrounds you. You gain 5 [[Temporary Hit Points]]. If a creature hits you with a melee attack roll before the spell ends, the creature takes 5 Cold damage. The spell ends early if you have no [[Temporary Hit Points]].

Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot. The [Temporary Hit Points](http://192.168.1.199:680/variantrules.html#temporary%20hit%20points_xphb) and the Cold damage both increase by 5 for each spell slot level above 1.
