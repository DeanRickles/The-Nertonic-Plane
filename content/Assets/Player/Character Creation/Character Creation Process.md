# Character Creation Process

### Character Creation
There is no limit on **races**, **backstory** or **spells**.  You can be anything in the standard 2024 rules no question. Anything outside of this needs approval.

#### Character Stats
Three options for selecting stats:
* Point buy
* Standard array.
* Roll Stats (4d6). 
	* Re-roll 1's.
	* Roll with DM present.
	* Rolls the stats our in order. (str, dex, con, wis, cha)
#### Banned feats
- Lucky
