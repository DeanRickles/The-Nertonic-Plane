**Key Terms and Concepts**
- Harvesting: The action of removing valuable materials (hides, glands, teeth) from a monster corpse.
- Monster Parts: The harvested materials often sold to alchemists, wizards, or specialized crafters.
- Butchering/Carving: Other common terms used for preparing a dead monster for sale, particularly for meat or hides. 

**Contextual Usage**
- Parts value: Parts from monsters are often valued based on their Challenge Rating (CR), with 4-8th level monster parts commonly valued around 200+ gp.
- Transaction: If selling a live captured beast, it is sometimes referred to as trading or, if using specific mechanics, selling at a high markup (e.g., 3-6 times the material value).
- Market: The sale of these items is often managed by specialized guilds or merchants, and may require licensing or haggling.
