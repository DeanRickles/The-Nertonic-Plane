## mi-high backstory
Mehigh was born beneath a blood-red eclipse, an omen the priests of the Ember Halo believed marked him for greatness. As an aasimar, he carried celestial blood in his veins, but unlike others of his kind, his gifts were strange. Fire answered him instinctively. Candles ignited when he prayed. Ash drifted around him when he grew angry. The elders called him the Flamebound Child.
He was raised within the sacred fortress-temple of the Purification Circle, an order that believed true purity could only be achieved through trial by fire. Their symbol — a ring of flame surrounding an empty center — represented the soul after all weakness had been burned away.
Mehigh believed in that creed completely.
He trained as both cleric and warrior, becoming one of the youngest guardians ever entrusted with the Circle’s sacred rites. Calm, loyal, and fiercely compassionate, he protected pilgrims, healed the wounded, and hunted creatures born from darkness. Many within the order believed he would one day lead them.
Then came the Night of Ash.
A hidden faction inside the order attempted a massive purification ritual beneath the cathedral of Vaelor. They claimed the world had grown too corrupt to save through mercy alone. The ritual used ancient celestial fire meant only for divine beings.
Mehigh tried to stop them.
The ritual shattered.
Holy flames erupted through the cathedral, turning stone to molten ruin. Mehigh dragged survivors from the inferno until the fire itself consumed half his face and armor. In the center of the blaze, he saw something no mortal was meant to witness — a living entity within the flames, vast and watching, speaking only one sentence:
“Purity demands sacrifice.”
When Mehigh awoke days later, the cathedral was gone, and the order blamed him for the disaster. Rather than reveal the corruption within their ranks, they branded him a traitor and cast him out.
But the fire never left him.
