## Background CONCEPT
#### Pre Deity
Born from [[The Sword Coast]].  Living in a small secluded town of Gnomes. Eldric Mornin the wizard of prestige knowledge, had read every book in the library.  Feeling starved for knowledge, until he found out there was a forbidden section in the library.  After weeks of planning, he broke into the forbidden section one evening and found an interesting book, one that showed the image of a skeleton hand with the roman numeral  'I'.  Sneaking the book home, and started to read the book to learn all it had to behold.

Weeks go past and the Gnome known to most as Eldric Mornin had read the book front to back etching the words into his mind.
One fateful night, was his reckoning. Door of his home knocked in by the the local enforcement. They had located the book using ??? spell after becoming aware of the books lack of existence in the library.

Exiled and betrayed by his brethren for learning Necromancy. In part his own fault.

Many moons pass. Some fruitful and others not so much.  Adventuring with a party of fellow likeminded nutters, Eldric Mornin ascended to his pinnace of skill. Craving for more Eldric Mornin cast Wish - To expand his Demi-plane to full fledged plane of existence. Starting working towards his accent on.

**Magic Resistance.** The necromancer has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
**Spellcasting.** The necromancer is a 20th-level spellcaster. Their spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 19, +11 to hit with spell attacks). The necromancer has the following wizard spells prepared:
 * **Cantrips (at will):** *chill touch* (4d8 necrotic damage), *mage hand*, *prestidigitation*, *ray of frost*, *toll the dead*
 * **1st level (4 slots):** *false life, mage armor, ray of sickness, shield*
 * **2nd level (3 slots):** *blindness/deafness, misty step, ray of enfeeblement*
 * **3rd level (3 slots):** *animate dead, counterspell, dispel magic, vampiric touch*

 * **6th level (2 slots):** *circle of death, create undead*
 * **7th level (2 slots):** *finger of death, forcecage*
 * **8th level (1 slot):** *abi-dalzim's horrid wilting, clone* (already cast)
 * **9th level (1 slot):** *power word kill*
### Actions
**Withering Touch.** *Melee Spell Attack:* +11 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 23 (4d8 + 5) necrotic damage.
**Command Undead (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).** The necromancer chooses one undead creature they can see within 60 feet of them. That creature must make a DC 19 Charisma saving throw. If it succeeds, the necromancer cannot use this feature on it again. If it fails, it becomes friendly to the necromancer and obeys their commands until the necromancer uses this feature again. Intelligent undead are harder to control; if the target has an Intelligence of 8 or higher, it has advantage on the saving throw. If it fails the saving throw and has an Intelligence of 12 or higher, it can repeat the saving throw at the end of every hour until it succeeds and breaks free.
### Legendary Actions
The necromancer can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The necromancer regains spent legendary actions at the start of their turn.
 * **Cantrip.** The necromancer casts a cantrip.
 * **Command Thrall.** The necromancer commands one undead creature under their control to use its reaction to move up to its speed and make one melee attack.
 * **Grave Magic (Costs 2 Actions).** The necromancer casts a spell of 3rd level or lower from their prepared spell list that has a casting time of 1 action.
 * **Frightful Gaze (Costs 2 Actions).** The necromancer fixes their gaze on one creature they can see within 10 feet of them. The target must succeed on a DC 19 Wisdom saving throw against this magic or become frightened for 1 minute. The frightened target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a target's saving throw is successful or the effect ends for it, the target is immune to the necromancer's gaze for the next 24 hours.


#### Lair Actions

On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), the necromancer takes a lair action to cause one of the following effects; the necromancer can’t use the same effect two rounds in a row:

• **Siphon Life Essence**: The necromancer targets one living creature they can see within 120 feet of them. The target must make a DC 19 Constitution saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. The necromancer regains hit points equal to half the amount of necrotic damage dealt.

• **Grasping Dead**: Skeletal hands or spectral claws erupt from the floors and walls in a 20-foot radius centered on a point the necromancer can see within 120 feet. All creatures of the necromancer's choice in that area must succeed on a DC 19 Strength saving throw or take 10 (3d6) bludgeoning damage and be restrained until initiative count 20 on the next round.

• **Aura of the Negative Plane**: The tower pulses with anti-life energy. Until initiative count 20 on the next round, any creature of the necromancer's choice within the lair cannot regain hit points. Additionally, any undead in the lair have advantage on saving throws against being turned during this time.

• **Macabre Mist**: A thick, freezing fog seeps from the stonework, filling a 40-foot-radius sphere centered on a point the necromancer chooses within the lair. The sphere spreads around corners, and its area is heavily obscured. A living creature that starts its turn in the fog takes 10 (3d6) cold damage. The fog lasts until the necromancer dismisses it as an action, uses this lair action again, or dies.

##### Regional Effects

A 20th-level necromancer's tower heavily pollutes the surrounding region with dark magic. The region containing the lair is warped by this magic, which creates one or more of the following effects:

• **Restless Dead**: Corpses buried within 1 mile of the lair have a high chance of animating on their own. Every night at midnight, there is a 50% chance that 1d6 zombies or skeletons dig their way out of their graves and begin wandering the area.

• **Rotting Flora**: Plant life within 1 mile of the tower is twisted and withered. Crops fail, trees are leafless and black, and foraging yields no edible food.

• **Chilling Atmosphere**: Within 1 mile of the tower, the temperature drops unnaturally, and shadows seem to lengthen and move on their own. Non-magical light sources only shed half their normal radius of bright and dim light.

If the necromancer dies, these effects fade over the course of 1d10 days.
