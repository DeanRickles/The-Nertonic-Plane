## Ruel’s Dagger: concept
#### Depiction
Double sided dagger. One blade with the word ‘IN’ and the other ‘OUT’, between the blades a  Leather  bound handle with a clear crystal mounted in the centre. 
#### Abilities
**In blade**. Draws out the soul of the target into the crystal in the blade, when dealing the last lethal blow.
**Out blade**. On an action, stabbing the target to curse the target with the soul stored in the crystal binding them. Giving the target polymorph to the creature cursed with.
**Crystal Polymorph**. As an action, polymorph into the stored soul. Until dispelled. 


## Description
Ruel was created once [[Mornin, The Soul Mage|Mornin]] ascended to Planar godhood, Ruel is the other side of [[Mornin, The Soul Mage|Mornin]].

Ruel’s authority on the plane is minor the same as all other God. They have apostle’s and may communicate with their apostles.

Ruel wishes to dam their creators world sending it into Chaos. They will use their tool so trickery to manipulate their apostles minds to believe they was tricked by the God of the plane to entering the plane on false pretences and instead should follow Ruel.

Ruel convinces their followers to cleanse the land of all souls is the only true way to send all the souls to their true descended plane.



### Depiction
The Image of Ruel is unknown, their visage is more of an idea of change for the better in Ruel's image.

Most know Ruel as the symbol of a flower dying of poison.
