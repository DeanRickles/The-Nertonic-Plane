# Lake of Bloom
Base in [[The Bloom Region (Center)]].


%% 
The lake of bloom is located in the centre of of the plane.

It is where all souls are brought before reforming into their organic forms.

The lake of bloom is created by the river trailing from [[Castle of Tears]] that Deity [[Eldath, God of Peace]], also known as The Mother of the Waters.
