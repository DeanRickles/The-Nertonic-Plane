[[Mornin, The Soul Mage|Eldric Mornin]] wished his patron [[Myrkul, God of Death]]  to assist him with a project to start a new demiplane. [[The Nertonic Plane]] which would be a place for a second chance in exchange Eldric promised to give Myrkul all his knowledge accumulated on his death or ascension to Godhood.

The plane was created hidden in the corner in the Ethereal Plane away from prying eyes.

# Location Types
There is the following types of locations:
* **City** - key location and all key shops.
* **Town** - housing and all key shops.
* **Village** - housing and 1-2 shops
* **Settlement** - Just people 
* **Landmarks** - Special locations.

### Classifications:
- **Settlement:** A general term for any place with human habitation.
- **Hamlet:** A very small settlement, often lacking services like shops or pubs.
- **Village:** A small rural settlement, usually having a few shops and a pub.
- **Town:** A medium-sized urban area, typically with 7,500 to 25,000+ inhabitants, serving as a regional center.
- **City:** A large, significant urban area. In the UK, this is a legal designation bestowed by the monarch, not strictly based on population, though they are usually populous, e.g., Birmingham.
- **Conurbation/Metropolis:** A large city and its surrounding suburbs, often holding over a million people.
