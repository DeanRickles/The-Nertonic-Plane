# Labyrinths

## Description 
Many scholars have written long, boring academic papers on the ins and outs of labyrinths. I’ll save you the drivel.

Labyrinths are multi-layered caverns and places of worship, filled with the magic of a bygone era that has accumulated to form its own unique ecosystem.

It has been found that when a labyrinth becomes active, it is a sign of magic surging due to a new magical item or tool being born.

There have been rare cases of creatures undergoing metamorphosis during these surges. If the dungeon isn’t conquered during these events, the residents will flee the labyrinth in fear, causing mayhem in the local area.

## Difficulty levels
Once a Labyrinth is located and chartered to its fullest content it is assigned by [[The Guild]]’s a level of difficulty is broke into categories to ensure the mortality rate of explorers are reduced. 

### Beginner
A party of 4 new adventures could complete with some experience. It is recommended on your first attempt to take a Labyrinths guide to ensure you don’t succumb to your demise.
